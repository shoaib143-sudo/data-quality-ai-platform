# Governance Maturity Assessment Health Decision

Date: 2026-09-29

Decision: governance maturity and assessment reliability are separate product concepts.

Governance Maturity continues to describe organizational capability. Assessment Health describes how strongly the displayed result is supported.

Assessment Health uses explicit visible inputs:

* 30% assessment completion
* 20% evidence-backed answered-question coverage
* 15% system-verification coverage
* 15% stakeholder depth, capped at four distinct respondents
* 10% multi-respondent consensus
* 10% evidence freshness

Bands are LOW below 45, MEDIUM from 45 through 74, and HIGH from 75.

Missing evidence or consensus receives no positive default. Assessment Health never modifies declared maturity.

Six synthetic organizational archetypes are retained as calibration evidence so policy-heavy, technically mature, AI-heavy, fragmented, early-stage, and mature regulated organizations do not collapse into the same result pattern.

This decision does not authorize production release.
