# DN-URA-1.0 Decision Record

Status: Approved product direction

## Approved decisions

- DN-URA is the default readiness onboarding direction for organizations.
- Data and AI readiness are assessed from day one, with explicit not-applicable handling.
- DataNexus automatically uses reliable connected evidence before asking factual questions.
- Authorized administrators may challenge observations with attributable evidence.
- DataNexus recommends target maturity; authorized administrators may override targets.
- Critical readiness gates may remain not cleared even when an aggregate score is high.
- Gaps can feed the existing governed recommendation, authorization/approval, execution, verification, and closure workflow.
- DN-URA is canonical. External frameworks appear only as supporting crosswalks/provenance.
- Readiness should become continuous and event-driven after onboarding.

## Guardrails

No framework mapping implies third-party certification or equivalence. Proprietary questionnaire wording is not copied into the canonical registry. Automated observations and human declarations remain separately attributable. Production migration, deployment, and destructive operations require their normal release controls.
