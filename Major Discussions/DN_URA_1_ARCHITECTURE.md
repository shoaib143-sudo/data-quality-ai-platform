# DN-URA-1.0 Architecture

## Why evidence-first

A conventional assessment repeatedly asks organizations for facts DataNexus may already observe. DN-URA instead evaluates canonical capabilities from normalized evidence and asks only the unresolved human-context questions.

## Core loop

Context -> Applicable capabilities -> Existing evidence -> Minimum unresolved questions -> Evidence reconciliation -> Readiness/gates -> Gaps -> Governed actions -> Verification -> New evidence.

## Capability over questionnaire

Capabilities are stable identifiers. Question wording, observation sources, framework mappings, and presentation can evolve without invalidating historical capability identity.

## Evidence hierarchy

System observations, human declarations, and documentary evidence remain distinct. Freshness and reliability affect confidence. Material disagreement between system and human evidence creates a contradiction finding.

## Lenses

Governance maturity, Data readiness, AI readiness, and use-case readiness are views over canonical capability state. They are not independent questionnaires.

## Safety

Readiness is not permission to execute. The Action Engine must pass proposed remediation through existing authorization and approval policy. A high aggregate score never overrides a failed critical capability gate.

## Evolution

The foundation begins with ten representative capabilities, one per dimension, plus MDM. The registry can expand toward the target 35-45 canonical capabilities without changing the engine contract.
