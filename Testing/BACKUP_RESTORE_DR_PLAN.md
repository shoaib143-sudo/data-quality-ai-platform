# Backup, Restore, and Disaster-Recovery Certification

## Automated certification

Provision controlled state, capture backup/recovery point using supported mechanisms, simulate loss in an isolated environment, restore, and verify database/storage/governance consistency.

## Assertions

Validate datasets and versions, policies, approvals, audit evidence, lineage, storage references, execution state, RLS/permissions, checksums/counts where applicable, and representative E2E journeys after restore.

Record recovery-point and recovery-time measurements when product objectives are defined. Restore success is not sufficient unless application invariants also pass.
